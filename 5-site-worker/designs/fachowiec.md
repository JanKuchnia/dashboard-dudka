## Overview
Plumber, electrician, builder, renovation, and the default for anything unlisted. Trustworthy, tidy, reliable. The visitor wants to know: do they do my job, in my area, and how do I reach them.

## Colors
- **Primary (#1F3A5F):** navy for headings, header and footer.
- **Accent (#E8762B):** warm orange for call buttons only.
- **Secondary (#EEF2F6):** alternating section backgrounds.
- **Surface (#FFFFFF):** page background.

## Typography
Confident bold sans-serif headings, clear readable body. No decorative fonts.

## Layout
- Hero: photo of finished work or the tradesperson at work, headline with the trade and town, orange call button, service area line.
- Services as a clean list or grid with one line each.
- Area served (towns) as plain text.
- Optional before/after or recent work photos.
- Contact block with phone, address and hours; sticky or repeated call button.
- Photos: realistic finished bathrooms, wiring, renovated rooms; bright daylight.
