## Overview
Guest house, pension, apartments, hostel. Quiet, inviting, restful. The surroundings and rooms sell the stay.

## Colors
- **Primary (#3D5A6C):** muted lake blue for buttons and headings.
- **Accent (#A9895B):** wood tone for small details.
- **Secondary (#EDF1EF):** pale sage section backgrounds.
- **Surface (#FBFBF9):** page background.

## Typography
Elegant serif headings with plenty of space; light, open sans-serif body.

## Layout
- Hero: full-width landscape or building photo, name and town, button to call and ask about availability.
- Rooms as large photos with short descriptions (prices only if known).
- Surroundings: what is nearby (mountains, lake, trails) if known.
- Amenities as a plain list (parking, breakfast, wifi) only if verified.
- Location, contact and how to get there at the bottom.
- Photos: wide natural landscapes, cosy rooms, morning light.
