## Overview
Dentist, physiotherapy, massage, veterinarian, optician. Calm, clean, reassuring. Lots of white space and soft colour; nothing loud or salesy.

## Colors
- **Primary (#2E6E68):** deep teal for buttons and headings.
- **Secondary (#E8F2F0):** pale mint section backgrounds.
- **Accent (#8FC1B5):** soft details only.
- **Surface (#FFFFFF):** page background.

## Typography
Rounded, friendly sans-serif throughout; comfortable line height.

## Layout
- Hero: bright photo of the practice or a calm treatment moment, headline with the specialty and town, button to book by phone.
- Services / treatments as a readable list with short plain-language explanations.
- A short section about the specialist or team if known.
- Hours, address and phone together; mention parking or access if known.
- Photos: bright clean rooms, gentle care, happy pets for vets, eyewear for opticians.
