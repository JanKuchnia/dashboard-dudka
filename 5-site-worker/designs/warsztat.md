## Overview
Car repair, tyres, car wash, locksmith. Solid, practical, no-nonsense. A customer with a problem should see what is fixed and how to call within two seconds.

## Colors
- **Primary (#F5B700):** call buttons and the phone number only. High-visibility yellow.
- **Secondary (#26282B):** panels and alternating sections.
- **Surface (#16171A):** dark page background.
- **Text (#F2F2F0):** body and headings.

## Typography
Heavy, wide sans-serif headings, sentence case or short uppercase labels. Phone number set very large.

## Layout
- Hero: photo of the workshop or work being done, headline saying what they fix, huge phone number button.
- Services as a clear two-column list or grid of short items.
- A short "why us" strip with concrete facts only (years, brands serviced) if known.
- Address with a map block and opening hours near the bottom; repeat the call button.
- Photos: real workshop scenes, tools, tyres, clean cars; strong contrast, not glossy stock.
