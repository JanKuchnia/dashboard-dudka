## Overview
Restaurant, pizzeria, fast food, cafe, bakery, patisserie. Appetising and generous. Food photography does most of the work.

## Colors
- **Primary (#B23A2B):** tomato red for buttons and key headings.
- **Accent (#E2A13B):** golden crust tone for small highlights.
- **Secondary (#F6EBDD):** warm cream section backgrounds.
- **Surface (#FFFAF3):** page background.

## Typography
Expressive, slightly soft serif display at large sizes; friendly sans-serif body.

## Layout
- Hero: full-width food photo with the name over it, buttons to call / order and see the menu.
- Signature dishes or products as large photos with names (prices only if known).
- Short story of the place in one paragraph.
- Opening hours prominent, address and how to get there.
- Photos: close, warm, overhead or 45° food shots; bakery/cafe gets pastries, bread, coffee.
