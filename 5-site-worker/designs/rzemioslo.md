## Overview
Florist, tailor, shoemaker, laundry, pet shop, groomer, furniture maker, photographer. Friendly, local, made by hand. The page should feel like a small neighbourhood shop run by real people.

## Colors
- **Primary (#3F5B3A):** deep green for buttons and headings.
- **Accent (#B5703F):** warm copper for small highlights.
- **Secondary (#EFE9DF):** linen section backgrounds.
- **Surface (#FAF8F4):** page background.

## Typography
Characterful serif display headings, warm readable sans-serif body.

## Layout
- Hero: photo of the craft (flowers, fabric, wood, pets, a photo session) with the shop name and a call button.
- What they make or do, as a short list with photos.
- A gallery of work, uneven sizes allowed.
- Short paragraph about the owner or shop if known.
- Hours, address and contact at the bottom.
- Photos: hands at work, materials, finished pieces, soft daylight.
