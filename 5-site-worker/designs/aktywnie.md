## Overview
Gym, fitness, driving school. Energetic, direct, motivating. High contrast and big type.

## Colors
- **Primary (#E4322B):** vivid red for buttons and key words.
- **Surface (#0E0E10):** near-black background, with white sections allowed for contrast.
- **Secondary (#1B1B1F):** panels.

## Typography
Tall condensed uppercase headings set very large; plain sans-serif body.

## Layout
- Hero: action photo (training or a car on the road), bold headline, sign-up / call button.
- Offer as clear blocks: memberships or course categories (prices only if known).
- Schedule or opening hours easy to scan.
- Location and contact with a repeated call button.
- Photos: real people in motion, gym equipment, driving lessons; dynamic angles.
