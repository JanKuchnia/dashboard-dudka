
## Do not (applies to every page)
- No row of three identical icon cards; use a list, a two-column zig-zag or an uneven grid instead.
- No generic centred hero with a stock gradient; put the business name, a real photo and the phone number in a split or left-aligned hero.
- No emojis, no neon or glowing shadows, no purple/blue AI gradients, no gradient text on headings, no pure black (#000000).
- No invented numbers or claims: no "500+ zadowolonych klientów", "15 lat doświadczenia", "100% satysfakcji" unless the brief gives them.
- No empty marketing phrases: "kompleksowe usługi", "najwyższa jakość", "profesjonalne podejście", "indywidualne podejście do klienta", "Twoja satysfakcja to nasz priorytet".
- No placeholder names or text (lorem ipsum, "Twoja firma", "Jan Kowalski" as a fake reviewer).
- No "scroll down" hints, bouncing arrows or custom cursors.
- No elements overlapping each other or text placed over busy photos without a solid backing.
- At most one primary call-to-action per section; the main one is calling the phone number.
